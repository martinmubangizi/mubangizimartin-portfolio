About

Kampala · Uganda

I started in land economics, valuing property so banks could judge what a loan was really secured by. That habit of asking what a number is worth followed me into card fraud on Stanbic Bank's VISA Risk desk, then into KYC, and now into mobile money at Wave.

Today I sit between data and compliance. I write SQL in Sigma on Snowflake, train models that spot agents gaming the distribution network, investigate cases with Compliance and report to the Bank of Uganda.

Outside work I build prediction engines for football, run Kazi Analytics for Ugandan sport, teach Python at Afro Fem Coders and study for an MBA at the University of East London.
