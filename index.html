Sanctions screening

Spelling changes.
The match doesn't.

Names arrive reordered, transliterated and half-typed. The matcher normalises them and compares three ways with Jaro-Winkler similarity. Drag the threshold and feel the trade-off between missed matches and false alarms.

What the matcher sees

Honorifics and legal suffixes are stripped, accents removed and word order ignored.
